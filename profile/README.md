# WaveShop - Edit a Recording You Made

At a glance:
- A waveform of audio you already have
- A selection you can hear before you keep it
- Undo until you write a new file
- The original take left in place

## The Waveform

WaveShop opens a file you can already play and draws the amplitude. You select silence at the head or a mistake in the middle and listen to that selection. If it is the part you want to remove, cut it on a copy. If it is the part you want to keep, trim to it and write a new name.

The screen is not the file until you save or export. Closing without saving should leave the source unchanged. Confirm that in Explorer if this recording is the only one. Do not learn the save dialog on the master.

## Selection Before Effects

Zoom until the cut is obvious. A wide view hides a click. Play the selection, then play across the join. A join that looks clean can still pop. Undo and move the edges. Effects, gain, and fades are optional and belong on the copy after the trim already sounds right.

WaveShop does not download music and does not remove protection from a store file. If the file will not open because it is locked, stop. Use a recording you made or a file you may edit.

## A New Name

Save As or export under a name that says it is the edit. Open that file in a normal player from the first second and the last. Keep the raw take in the original folder. A later mix can start from the take even if this edit was wrong.

Do not stack three effects before the first export exists. You will not know which step caused a problem. One change, one file, one listen.

## What Stays

The project of this tool is the audio file, not a separate session you can relink forever. That is why the original filename must survive. Delete an experiment only after the player has played the new file and you know you will not need the longer take.

## Undo While It Is Open

Undo applies to the edit in memory. After you overwrite a file, undo may not bring the old bytes back from disk. This is why the new name matters. If you already saved over the only take, stop and look for a backup before you edit again.

## Hear It Elsewhere

The waveform view can make a quiet section look like silence when a speaker would still play a hiss. Listen on the device you will actually use. Then decide. WaveShop will not master a song you do not have rights to use, and a commercial track is not a bed just because it fits.

## Player Profile

WaveShop fits a trim of a take you recorded. It does not fit a protected file, a watermark you did not make, or a save onto the only original before you have heard the result elsewhere.

## Help Desk

The join pops? Undo, move the selection, and export again from the copy.
You saved over the take? Stop. Restore a backup before another edit.
The file will not open? It may be locked or not audio you can edit. Do not try to strip protection.
Effects made it worse? Delete that export and return to the unprocessed copy.
The screen looks silent but you hear hiss? Trust your ears and keep the span you need.

## Reader Notes

A short recording teaches the selection and the new name faster than an hour of unsorted audio. Keep the raw file in a folder named so you will not export on top of it.

Listen outside the editor. The waveform is a guide. The file you send is the one a normal player opens. Until that listen is done, both copies stay.

WaveShop is a wave editor, not a download tool and not a mixer for a catalog. Bring the audio you already have. Leave with a new file and the original.
